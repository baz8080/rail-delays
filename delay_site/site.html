How to read a disruption

Each entry names the service or the stretch of line the notice is about, when it was first listed, and how often it was re-worded while it stood. Under that are Irish Rail's own words: the headline first, then the sentence beneath it. The tags are what those words named, and a notice can name more than one thing.

Where a notice claims a figure, the entry carries the worst figure that notice ever claimed, prefixed "at least". The feed writes ranges - "+15/20 minutes delayed" - and a range is only a promise at its lower end.
